# Compare

Trigger: ` vs `, ` versus `, `compare `. The engine splits the brief, strips each side, and runs the **same** source set on `a` and `b`.

Default sources: LinkedIn people, LinkedIn companies, YouTube. Table has a `side` column. Do not write a blog-post verdict. Paste the table. If they want a narrative, three bullets after the table: who showed up only on A, only on B, on both.

Two-way only. `A vs B vs C` is two finds, or they pick the pair.

Scores are still presence. Do not declare a winner from YouTube views on one side and LinkedIn snippets on the other.
